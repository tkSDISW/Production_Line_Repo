## Process: verify exchange-item naming convention before rolling out to remaining stations

**Status:** Active

The `<Component>.<event>` exchange-item naming convention (DEC-0007) has been applied to all 30 items across Master, Infeed Load, and Filling Station and pushed (capella-fabric commit d2f56404). Before applying the same convention to the remaining 3 planned RPL replicas (Capping, Labeling/Inspection, Case Packing per DEC-0001), the user wants to confirm in Capella itself that the renamed items actually resolve the exchange-item allocation picker confusion as intended — i.e. verify the fix works in practice on the two done so far, rather than assuming it and batch-applying to all five stations up front.

**Process going forward:** as each new station is created and disconnected from the REC, name its exchange items with the same convention individually (or in small batches), rather than waiting to do all remaining stations in one large patch. This keeps the risk of a systemic naming mistake contained to one station at a time.
